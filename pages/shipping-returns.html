Delivery & Shipping

We use Parcelforce courier for UK and DHL courier for international deliveries. We offer next day delivery on in stock orders within the UK if ordered by 10am.

Production time

Every piece is cut and sewn to order in our own factory in Greece. Custom orders are custom made for you and can take up to 15 days to arrive. Pre-orders are due into delivery within 15 days as well. Accessories and gift vouchers are the exception — those ship the same or next working day.

UK delivery

  • Orders placed before 10am Monday to Friday will be delivered the next working day.
  • Orders placed after 10am will be delivered within 2 days.
  • Orders placed after 10am on Friday will be delivered on Tuesday, unless the Saturday delivery option has been chosen.

All deliveries must be signed for and are sent tracked so we can ensure you have received your item. You will get a tracking link by email as soon as it leaves us.

Tax and duties

International shipping does not include custom or duty fees. The customer is responsible for paying any fees upon delivery. These fees can vary for each country, so we recommend contacting your local customs office before ordering for estimated costs or to ask any questions.

If you reject the parcel by refusing to pay custom and duty fees, the return shipping cost will be deducted from your refund.

Packaging

  • Every order arrives in our rigid red box with the matching rope-handle carrier.
  • Nothing is ever sent in a poly bag.
  • Free gift wrapping and a handwritten note on request — just say so in the order notes.

Returns

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery on pieces that are unworn, unwashed and still have their tags on. Return shipping is on us for UK orders; elsewhere it is yours, though we will always give you the cheapest label we can find.

  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 working days of the return arriving.
  • Original delivery cost is not refunded.
  • Exchanges for a different size are free in the UK.

Exceptions

  • Bespoke and made-to-measure pieces are final sale. They are made for you specifically.
  • Deposits are fully refundable until cutting starts, then non-refundable.
  • Gift vouchers are non-refundable but can be exchanged for a higher value.
  • Sale items follow the same 30-day policy unless marked final sale on the product page.

Faults

Every piece is checked by hand before it is boxed. If something is wrong with it, tell us and we will repair or replace it, and cover the postage both ways. That is not a legal obligation we are negotiating — it is what we would want as a customer.

Shipping somewhere unusual?

We ship worldwide, but if your country is not listed above, ask and we will check the cost and timing for you.
