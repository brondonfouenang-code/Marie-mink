Getting the fit right

Fur should never feel tight.

The most common fitting mistake is going down a size because it is “a coat”. It will not go over what you already wear. Measure first, and if you are between sizes, size up.

How to measure

Use a soft tape and measure over the layers you will actually wear underneath. Keep it snug but not tight.

  • Chest — around the fullest part, under the arms, tape level all the way round.
  • Shoulder — seam to seam across the back, following the shoulder line.
  • Sleeve — from the shoulder seam to the wrist bone, with the arm slightly bent.
  • Back length — from the base of the neck down to where you want the hem to fall.

Where should the hem fall?

  • Short (110 cm coat): mid-thigh. Works over a dress or a suit, looks clean with jeans.
  • Long (115 cm coat): just below the knee. The most versatile length for winter.
  • Very long: mid-calf. Dressier, but check you can walk in heels comfortably.
