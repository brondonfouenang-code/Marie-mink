How it works at checkout

  1. Add your piece and choose your colour and size as normal.
  2. At the payment step you will see Klarna and Clearpay alongside the cards.
  3. Choose “Pay in 6 interest-free instalments”. You will be taken to the provider to confirm.
  4. We start production as soon as the first payment clears.

Worked example

On a coat priced at £1,500, six payments of £250. The total you pay is £1,500 — there is no interest and no fee added by us. The provider may check eligibility before confirming, which is their process rather than ours.

Good to know

  • Instalment plans are arranged with a third-party provider, not by us. Their terms apply.
  • We never see or store your payment details — they go straight to the provider.
  • Deposit orders are non-refundable once cutting has started, but fully refundable before.
  • Business and EU customers can ask for a proforma invoice instead. Just ask.

Which country can I order from?
