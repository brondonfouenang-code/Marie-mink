How our own six month plan works

  1. Message us on WhatsApp and tell us which coat you want, and your size.
  2. Pay the £50 deposit. Your coat is reserved.
  3. Pay the balance off over up to six months, at your own pace.
  4. Once it is paid, your coat is made and sent, tracked and signed for.

Good to know

  • Klarna and Clearpay are third-party providers. Their terms and approval apply, not ours.
  • We never see or store your payment details — they go straight to the provider.
  • With our own plan, nothing is sent until the balance is paid in full.

Which country can I order from?
