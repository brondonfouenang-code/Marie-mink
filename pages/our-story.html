Since 2024

We stopped reselling and started making.

Most fur sold online is a middleman. We wanted to know exactly which pelt went into your coat — so we bought the factory.

The problem with buying fur online

If you have ever bought a "mink" coat online, the odds are you could not tell what species it was, where the pelts came from, or whether the lining was silk or polyester. The listing said real fur. The listing is not inspected by anybody. You were.

We started Marie Minks because we got tired of that. The whole business was built around one idea: remove the distance between the person choosing the fur and the person cutting it.

Our own factory in Greece

Since 2024 we have had our own production floor. That means we buy the pelts at the European auctions ourselves, we choose which lot goes into which piece, and we cut to order rather than out of a warehouse of pre-made coats.

  • We attended the 2025 Saga Furs auction and bought from the top lots.
  • Every pelt is selected by hand before it reaches the cutting table.
  • Cut and sewn to your size, not pulled from stock.
  • One maker signs off on every finished piece before it is boxed.

Saga Furs Top Lot, 2025

Top lot at a Saga auction is not a marketing phrase — it is a graded category. The pelts are the largest, the cleanest and the most uniform in colour, which is exactly what makes a coat look expensive rather than furry. When the auction moved to selecting only the best, most of what you see on cheaper sites disappeared from the market entirely.

Why made to order takes ten days

Because we cut when you order. A coat that sat in a warehouse for a year has been handled, stored, and pressed repeatedly — and you can feel it in the nap. Cutting yours the day it comes in is slower for us and much better for you.

The part we did not plan

Most of our customers find us on WhatsApp, and most of them write because they are nervous. Is this too long? Is this colour too pale? Will it fit over a coat? Those are better questions than a size chart, so we answer them — usually the same day, by a person, for free.

That is the whole business. Top lot fur, our own hands, and someone who will tell you the truth.
