Is this real fur, and how do I know?

Yes. Everything except our reindeer trims is real fur: mink, fox, and in the case of the teddy bears, genuine mink cut from coat offcuts.

You can check it yourself before you buy. Fur has a visible root end, it does not melt like polyester, and a real pelt cannot be peeled off layer by layer. The lining is silk or quilted satin — if a listing says “fur-like” or “faux”, it is not ours.

Why is it made to order?

Because we cut when you order rather than pulling coats out of storage. It takes 10 to 14 days, and it is why the nap looks the way it does on arrival — fresh, not flattened by a year of being pressed against other coats in a warehouse.

It also means we do not have to stock sizes that will not sell, which is part of why our prices are what they are.

How do I care for a fur coat?
  • Hang it on a broad, padded hanger. Never a wire one.
  • Keep it out of direct sunlight and away from heat.
  • Do not wash it at home, ever.
  • Once a season, take it to a specialist furrier for a clean and re-nap.
  • Use cedar or mothballs in the closet, refreshed each season.

Real fur needs far less maintenance than most people expect — it does not need dry cleaning every year.

Will it last?

A good mink coat should last thirty years or more with proper care. Our customers write to us about pieces they bought a decade ago that still look new.

The thing that ages fur fastest is heat and sunlight, not wear. Treat it like a good coat, not like a costume.

How do I know which size?

Measure over the layers you will wear underneath, and size up if you are between sizes. Fur should never feel tight across the shoulders.

The full chart is on the size guide. Better still, send us a photo of a coat you already own on WhatsApp and we will tell you which is closest.

What about the environmental question?

It is a fair question and we do not hide from it. Fur is a natural, renewable, fully biodegradable material from animals raised for food — unlike plastic-based faux fur, which sheds microfibres and lasts a fraction of the time. Our pieces come from animals raised in the EU for meat, from farms subject to welfare law, and we buy from the top grades precisely so that fewer, better pelts are used.

If you have decided against real fur and want a quality alternative, say so and we will point you to a good one rather than sell you something you do not want.
